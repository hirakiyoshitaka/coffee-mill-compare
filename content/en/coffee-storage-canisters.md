## What not to buy

- **A clear jar with a loose lid.** Light and air are what stale beans. A canister that only looks airtight is a jar, not storage.
- **A canister far bigger than the bag.** A half-empty canister holds half a canister of air. Match the size to the bag — the table below starts from that.
- **A second copy of a canister they already own.** Storage they already use is not a gift, even in a nicer color.
