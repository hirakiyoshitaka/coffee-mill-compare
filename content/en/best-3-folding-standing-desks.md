I am a 58-year-old office worker. Some days the desk has to come out, and some days I want the room back. The desk I use folds away when it is in the way, and it rises high enough to work standing. That has been very convenient. I do not know the brand, and I am not naming a model.

The three below are review summaries. They are official numbers for desks that fold and that publish a standing-height range. They are not a claim that I own these model numbers. I am not describing how the lever feels.

1. Sanwa Direct 100-ERD054. A review summary
2. Sanwa Direct 100-ERD044. A review summary
3. Sanwa Direct 100-DESKN002. A review summary. The announcement is from 2018

All three are from the same maker. I put the ones whose fold thickness and standing range are written on an official page first. Checked on November 2, 2026.

## 1. 100-ERD054, a review summary

From Sanwa Direct's [February 26, 2026 announcement](https://direct.sanwa.co.jp/contents/news/item/100-ERD054BK.html) and the [100-ERD054W product page](https://direct.sanwa.co.jp/ItemPage/100-ERD054W).

- Gas lift, stepless from 74 to 108.2 cm. Top W70×D48 cm. Load 30 kg
- The legs fold. The announcement says about 15 cm thick when folded. The product page lists about 11.6 kg. It ships assembled
- The top tilts up to 90 degrees, with a stop against things sliding off, and a hook
- The black model's announced price was 18,910 yen before tax. Check the live page. A white-page price moves with sales, so I am not locking one in

If folded thickness is the first question, open this model. Whether 108.2 cm meets your standing elbow height is something the page does not map to a body height.

[Search for the 100-ERD054 on Amazon.co.jp](https://www.amazon.co.jp/s?k=100-ERD054&tag=stocknotelife-22)

## 2. 100-ERD044, a review summary

From the [100-ERD044BK product page](https://direct.sanwa.co.jp/ItemPage/100-ERD044BK). The heading says 16 cm thick.

- About 69 cm wide, 49.7 cm deep, 75.1–109.5 cm high. About 13 kg. Load 30 kg
- Gas lift. Stepless top angle. Casters. The feature list includes a tablet stand, a cup holder, a hook, and a slide stop
- Ships assembled. Made in China. The spec table says the warranty covers initial defects only
- List price is open. I am not copying the day's selling price

The top of the range is about 1 cm higher than the 054. The holders are the other difference to read.

[Search for the 100-ERD044 on Amazon.co.jp](https://www.amazon.co.jp/s?k=100-ERD044&tag=stocknotelife-22)

## 3. 100-DESKN002, a review summary

The source is Sanwa Supply's [announcement of October 9, 2018](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001614.000011495.html). Confirm that it is still sold. I am not promising stock.

- Top height 790–1,185 mm. Overall about W790×D530×H802–1,197 mm. About 15.2 kg
- The announcement says it folds to 192 mm thick
- Top W790×D480 mm. A foot pedal changes the height. A lever under the top tilts it from flat to 90 degrees downward
- Two casters at the rear

If you only compare the highest standing figure, 1,185 mm is the largest of the three. The folded thickness, 192 mm, is thicker than the 054's about 15 cm.

[Search for the 100-DESKN002 on Amazon.co.jp](https://www.amazon.co.jp/s?k=100-DESKN002&tag=stocknotelife-22)
