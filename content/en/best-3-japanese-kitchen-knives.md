I am a 58-year-old office worker, and I live in Japan. I have not used a high-end gyuto. I am not describing how they cut. This page only narrows a first knife by what the makers publish. All three are review summaries.

1. Misono UX10 gyuto No.712 (210 mm). A review summary
2. Seki Magoroku Damascus gyuto 210 mm (AE5205). A review summary
3. Tojiro Classic gyuto 210 mm (F-808). A review summary

For a home board, 210 mm sat in the middle of the lengths these catalogs list. Each maker also has 180 mm, and longer blades from 240 mm up. I do not have a length preference of my own.

## 1. Misono UX10 gyuto No.712, a review summary

From [Misono's UX10 catalog](https://www.misono-hamono.com/UX10/santoku.html). The page gives the head-office factory as 27 Obuto-cho, Seki, Gifu.

- Gyuto runs from No.711 at 180 mm, through No.712 at 210 mm, then 240, 270, and 300 mm
- That catalog page did not list steel, weight, price, or handle material. I am not filling those in
- What I could confirm is a 210 mm gyuto in a series whose maker prints a Seki factory address

[Search for the Misono UX10 210 mm gyuto on Amazon.co.jp](https://www.amazon.co.jp/s?k=%E3%83%9F%E3%82%BD%E3%83%8E+UX10+%E7%89%9B%E5%88%80+210&tag=stocknotelife-22)

Other numbers show up in search. Check for No.712.

## 2. Seki Magoroku Damascus 210 mm, a review summary

From [Kai's Seki Magoroku Damascus page](https://www.kai-group.com/products/brand/sekimagoroku/knives/damascus/). The page calls the line an upper series.

- Gyuto 210 mm is model AE5205. Blade length 210 mm. 18,700 yen including tax
- Kai describes the blade as a Damascus pattern of different stainless steels folded into 32 layers
- The handle is laminated reinforced wood, on a shaft without rivets. The bolster is welded and polished
- There is also 180 mm, AE5204, at 17,600 yen including tax. The page did not give a Rockwell hardness

[Search for the Seki Magoroku Damascus AE5205 on Amazon.co.jp](https://www.amazon.co.jp/s?k=%E9%96%A2%E5%AD%AB%E5%85%AD+%E3%83%80%E3%83%9E%E3%82%B9%E3%82%AB%E3%82%B9+AE5205&tag=stocknotelife-22)

## 3. Tojiro Classic 210 mm, a review summary

From [Tojiro's official shop](https://store.tojiro.net/products/f-808). The shop heading says the knives are made in Tsubame-Sanjo. Model F-808. The price that day was 14,300 yen including tax.

- Blade about 210 mm. Overall about 335 mm. Weight about 200 g
- The shop says the blade is VG10 core with 13-chrome stainless
- Handle of laminated reinforced material. Double bevel, for right- or left-handed use, according to the table
- That product page did not give a hardness number

[Search for the Tojiro F-808 on Amazon.co.jp](https://www.amazon.co.jp/s?k=%E8%97%A4%E6%AC%A1%E9%83%8E+F-808&tag=stocknotelife-22)

## How I would narrow them, using only the pages

Steel and weight are actually written down for Seki Magoroku's 32 layers, and for Tojiro's VG10 and about 200 g. For Misono, the catalog I opened stopped at the Seki factory and a 210 mm blade. I am not ranking sharpness. Dishwasher notes were not lined up the same way on these three pages, so read each maker's care line before you buy.
