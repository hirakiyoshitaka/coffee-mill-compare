## 1. De'Longhi Magnifica Start, a review summary

This is the full-auto for someone who wants milk drinks from a button. Sources: [De'Longhi Japan's product page](https://www.delonghi.com/ja-jp/p/magnifica-start-ecam22080gb-magunifuikasuta%E3%83%BCto-%E5%85%A8%E8%87%AA%E5%8B%95ko%E3%83%BChi%E3%83%BCmashin/ECAM22080GB.html) and the [ECAM22080 manual](https://dls.delonghigroup.com/files/pdfmanuals/JP-57132C9794.pdf).

- The product page listed a maximum pump pressure of 15 bar, 100 V, 1,450 W, and a weight field of 9.2. The manual says about 9.5 kg. Both numbers stay, because they disagree
- The manual's size is about W240×D440×H350 mm. Water tank 1.8 L. Bean hopper 250 g. Conical grinder. Grounds container up to 15 cups
- Drinks: espresso, a "specialty" coffee, cappuccino, latte macchiato, and hot water. Milk is LatteCrema Hot
- The price on the product page that day was 124,800 yen. It changes
- The manual says the machine is for Japan only, and not for a different supply voltage

A wider look at newer grinder machines, in English, is [Breville and De'Longhi](/en/articles/breville-vs-delonghi-all-in-one-espresso/). I have not used those either. The Japanese budget guide is [home latte and espresso](/articles/home-latte-espresso-machines/).

## 2. BALMUDA The Brew, a review summary

For someone who already grinds, and who wants to watch the pour. There is no mill. Sources: [specifications](https://www.balmuda.com/jp/brew/spec) and [features](https://www.balmuda.com/jp/brew/).

- Model K06A. Open drip. Modes REGULAR, STRONG, ICED, and Cleaning
- Body W140×D297×H379 mm including the handle. About 3.4 kg. AC 100 V, 1,450 W. Cord 1 m
- Carafe about 500 ml, tank about 490 ml. One to three cups. REGULAR and STRONG about 120 / 240 / 360 ml. ICED about 75 / 150 / 225 ml. About 4–7 minutes
- BALMUDA describes drips in 0.2 ml steps and a bypass pour. The how-to says to use medium-fine to medium grounds
- Made in China. For use in Japan only. Warranty is one year from purchase, or two years from BALMUDA's own online store, according to the spec table
- That spec table did not list a suggested retail price in yen

## 3. TWINBIRD CM-D457, a review summary

Bean-to-cup drip for one or two people. Source: [TWINBIRD's official store](https://store.twinbird.jp/products/cmd457).

- The price shown that day was 41,800 yen including tax. The heading mentions a five-year warranty limited to the official store
- About 160×335×360 mm. About 4.1 kg. AC 100 V, 50–60 Hz. 610 W. Maximum 450 ml (3 cups). Cord 1.4 m
- A slow flat-burr mill, a six-way shower, and brew temperatures of 83°C and 90°C. The page says the keep-warm temperature is 70°C
- The page credits supervision by Mamoru Taguchi. I have not brewed with it, so I am not grading the cup

[Search for the TWINBIRD CM-D457 on Amazon.co.jp](https://www.amazon.co.jp/s?k=%E3%83%84%E3%82%A4%E3%83%B3%E3%83%90%E3%83%BC%E3%83%89+CM-D457&tag=stocknotelife-22)

## Which setup

Milk drinks from one press: Magnifica Start. You already grind and want to watch a drip: The Brew. Beans in, up to three cups of drip: CM-D457. Two of them are 1,450 W and the TWINBIRD is 610 W. Read each manual's note on the outlet.
