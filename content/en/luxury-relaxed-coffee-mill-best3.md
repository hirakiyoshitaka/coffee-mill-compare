## 3. Comandante C40 MK4 — review summary

I have not used a Comandante C40. I am not writing how it turns, or how it feels in the hand. It is on this list for design and a premium feel, so this section only repeats what this site already took from the official pages opened on October 31, 2026.

- The product page says it is made in Germany, and that the burr is the Nitro Blade, a high-nitrogen steel. The maker says it resists wear and needs less care
- Adjustment is a three-legged dial on the bottom. Zero is where the burrs meet, and you count the clicks you open from there. The official description does not include a numbered ring
- One glass jar and one polymer jar are included. Each holds 40g
- The official product page opened that day did not list grams, or a price in yen
- This page does not say whether it tastes better or worse than the Timemore

The longer comparison is [Comandante C40 vs Timemore Chestnut S5 Pro](/en/articles/comandante-c40-vs-timemore/).
