These three are for a morning you do not want to rush, with a hand grinder that feels a little more special. I look at design and a premium feel. I use the first two. Only the third is a short summary of what the maker publishes, not a note from my hands.

1. Timemore Chestnut S5 Pro. This is the one on my counter in the morning
2. EPEIOS Essence Go. The other one I own. The one line I can write is that it just spins so easily
3. Comandante C40 MK4. A review summary. I have not used it

This is not a scored taste ranking. Prices are approximate.

## 1. Timemore Chestnut S5 Pro

The one I keep for the morning is the green model, with a folding handle. I like the design. It has a solid, premium weight, and it is not too heavy. I like this color.

### Impressions from actually using it (owner review)

The beans are from [Coffee Carrot (珈琲きゃろっと)](/beans/coffee-carrot/). That shop page is in Japanese.

- Grinding enough for two cups takes about a minute.
- It grinds with a light effort. I change the grind by clicking the outer dial, and making the cup stronger or lighter is genuinely easy.
- The handle folds, so it stores compactly.
- Taking it apart to clean is very easy.
- I like the design. It has a solid, premium weight, and it is not too heavy. I like the green.
- Grinding Coffee Carrot beans with the Timemore makes that coffee time feel like bliss.
- I have no particular complaints. A finer grind does get reasonably heavier to turn.
- With some beans, static builds up, and brushing the grounds into the dripper with the included brush is a little tedious.

If you want that unhurried morning to start with this kind of coffee time, look here first. The longer page is the [Chestnut S5 Pro review](/en/articles/timemore-chestnut-s5-pro/).

## 2. EPEIOS Essence Go

Second is the other grinder I own, the EPEIOS Essence Go.

## 3. Comandante C40 MK4 — review summary

I have not used a Comandante C40. I am not writing how it turns, or how it feels in the hand. It is on this list for design and a premium feel, so this section only repeats what this site already took from the official pages opened on October 31, 2026.

- The product page says it is made in Germany, and that the burr is the Nitro Blade, a high-nitrogen steel. The maker says it resists wear and needs less care
- Adjustment is a three-legged dial on the bottom. Zero is where the burrs meet, and you count the clicks you open from there. The official description does not include a numbered ring
- One glass jar and one polymer jar are included. Each holds 40g
- The official product page opened that day did not list grams, or a price in yen
- This page does not say whether it tastes better or worse than the Timemore

The longer comparison is [Comandante C40 vs Timemore Chestnut S5 Pro](/en/articles/comandante-c40-vs-timemore/).

Search results mix in other model numbers. Check that the title says MK4 and Nitro Blade.
