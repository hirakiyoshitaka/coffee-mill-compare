I am a 58-year-old office worker. On a weekend round, one wrong yard is enough to make the club choice feel unsettled. The rangefinder in my bag now is Nikon's COOLSHOT PROIII STABILIZED.

The order, first:

1. Nikon COOLSHOT PROIII STABILIZED. The one I use
2. Bushnell Pro X3+LINK. A review summary. I have not used it
3. Voice Caddie SL3. A review summary. I have not used it

This is not a scored contest. Shake used to move the pin around in the view for me, so the stabilized Nikon comes first. The other two are a short reading of each maker's own page, checked on November 2, 2026.

## 1. Nikon COOLSHOT PROIII STABILIZED

I used a different laser rangefinder before. I am not naming that model. After I switched to the COOLSHOT PROIII STABILIZED, the stabilization cut the shake by a lot, and the distance it showed was the one I was aiming at. That alone made golf feel better.

The lines below are Nikon's own description, not more of my round.

- Nikon's heading calls it the fastest in the COOLSHOT series at 0.1 seconds, and the top stabilized model. On sale April 25, 2025. Open price
- STABILIZED reduces shake in the view and also steadies the laser, in Nikon's words
- HYPER READ shows a result in 0.1 seconds regardless of the distance
- Range 7.5–1,090 m / 8–1,200 yd. Magnification 6x. Size 102×74×42 mm. About 205 g without the battery
- Straight-line accuracy is ±0.75 m/yd. under 700, ±1.25 from 700 up to 1,000, and ±1.75 at 1,000 and beyond. Nikon says the target and the weather can keep it from meeting those figures
- Dual LOCKED ON QUAKE uses a sign and a vibration when it reads the pin. Slope is ID TECHNOLOGY
- Waterproofing is a design that can sit at 1 m for 10 minutes, and it is not for use underwater. The battery chamber is water-resistant for daily life. The body is fogproof. Power is one CR2

Sources: [overview](https://nij.nikon.com/products/lineup/laser/coolshot_pro3_stabilized/) and [specifications](https://nij.nikon.com/products/lineup/laser/coolshot_pro3_stabilized/spec.html). There is no photo here. I do not have a picture I took of my own unit.
