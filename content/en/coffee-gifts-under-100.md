## What not to buy

- **A blade grinder.** It chops the beans instead of grinding them to an even size, so the cup comes out uneven. The picks on this page are scales, brewers, and storage — none of them needs a blade grinder to work.
- **A second copy of gear they already own.** A scale they already weigh with is not a gift, even in a nicer color. Match the gift to what they do not have yet — the table below starts from that.
