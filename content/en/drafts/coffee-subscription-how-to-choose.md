## How to choose a coffee subscription starts with what arrives, and where it can be sent

A coffee subscription is a form of coffee, and a country it will actually ship to, before it is a price.

I looked up the three shops in the Japanese draft on November 3, 2026. Each official page limits delivery to Japan:

- PostCoffee’s shipping help says they do not ship products overseas. The page also says the subscription ships with postage included, inside that same domestic service.
- Doi Coffee’s delivery guide says delivery is for addresses inside Japan only.
- Coffee Carrot’s shopping guide says delivery is inside Japan only. The page’s own stamp says it was updated November 1, 2026.

A straight translation of those three checkouts would read as if a reader in the United States, the United Kingdom, or elsewhere could sign up. They cannot, on the pages above. I have not used PostCoffee or Doi Coffee. I am not pasting customer comments from any of the shops.

## Bean, ground, or bags, against the gear already on the counter

The first split is whole bean, ground, or a drip bag. Price comes after the question of whether the kitchen can brew what arrives.

Whole bean needs a mill. The grinder on my counter is the [Timemore Chestnut S5 Pro](/en/articles/timemore-chestnut-s5-pro/). Another candidate is the [EPEIOS Essence Go](/en/articles/epeios-essence-go/). How I would choose a hand mill is the [hand-grinder guide](/en/articles/how-to-choose-hand-coffee-mill/). Hand versus electric is a [separate page](/en/articles/hand-vs-electric-coffee-mill/). Where to set the dial, once a mill is in the house, is the [grind-size guide](/en/articles/coffee-grind-size-guide/).

Ground coffee does not need a mill. It still needs a dripper and a filter. I am not linking a dripper page here, because this site’s English articles do not have one yet.

A drip bag needs a cup and hot water. No mill, and no dripper.

The next thing I look for is whether a month I cannot finish can be skipped, and whether the service asks for a minimum number of orders before it can stop.

## What to read on the service’s own page

Five lines, on that service’s site, before a recurring charge:

1. The form: whole bean, a named grind, or a bag that brews in the cup. If the page does not list the form, the cell stays blank.
2. The countries. A service that names one country is not a choice in the countries it leaves out.
3. The price as printed, including whether shipping is inside that price. A crossed-out trial price is not the same number as a single bag.
4. The skip or pause deadline, in days, not only the word “anytime.”
5. A minimum number of deliveries, if the page has one.

I am not naming a one-time trial set for readers outside Japan. Doi Coffee’s small trial set is part of the Japanese draft, and that shop ships inside Japan only.

No service I could verify on November 3, 2026 ships to every country this site is written for. The two below are unused. They are a review roundup. They are not a ranking, and they are not a pair to buy together.

## Trade Coffee, on the pages that limit it to the United States

I have not ordered from Trade.

The subscribe page, opened November 3, 2026, says Trade ships within the United States by USPS, to all 50 states, including Alaska and Hawaii. The same answer says they do not ship to APO, FPO, or DPO addresses, Puerto Rico, Canada, or internationally, and they do not ship to freight-forwarding addresses. The terms page says the site may be opened from outside the United States, and that Trade does not currently ship products outside the United States. I am leaving both sentences in. Puerto Rico is named on the subscribe page even though the terms sentence says “outside the United States.”

The subscribe page says a subscription roasts and ships every two weeks, and that the interval can be changed from the dashboard after the first delivery. It also says they ask how you brew. A list of grind settings, and any drip bag, was not in the text I saved from that page or from the collection page at drinktrade.com/products/best-sellers. 【Not on the pages opened: Trade grind settings, and whether a drip bag is offered】

Prices, only as printed that day, and not added into a year:

- The collection page at drinktrade.com/products/best-sellers showed $21.99 per standard 10.9 oz bag.
- The same page showed a three-bag trial line of $43.98, with $65.97 struck through, first bag free, and free shipping with the trial.
- The terms page says a rotating selection such as The Hookup ranges from $12 to $22, excluding tax and shipping.

Those are different offers. I am not picking one as the price of “Trade,” and I am not multiplying either by 12. Another page I did not use for this draft may show a different bag price. The checkout is the number that matters when someone actually subscribes.

The terms also say a subscription may not be cancelled or changed after the next shipment has been processed, on the roaster’s schedule, up to 10 days before the delivery date. The subscribe page did not give a calendar day such as “the 20th.” 【Not on the subscribe page: a fixed day-of-month deadline】

A minimum number of deliveries was not stated on the pages I opened. 【Not confirmed: a minimum number of Trade orders】

## Pact Coffee, limited to the delivery area written in its terms

I have not ordered from Pact.

The terms page says Pact can only guarantee delivery in mainland England, Scotland and Wales, Northern Ireland, and the Channel Islands. I did not find a sentence that accepts or refuses an address in Ireland, the rest of Europe, the United States, or Australia. 【Not on the terms page opened: orders outside that list】

The how-it-works page lists whole bean, plus fine, medium-fine, medium, and coarse, tied to brew methods (espresso, AeroPress or moka, V60 or other drip filters, cafetière or cold brew). Drip bags were not on that list.

Prices on November 3, 2026 did not agree with each other, so both stay:

- The Select plan page: £8.95 per 250 g bag.
- The how-it-works page: House £8.95 per bag, Select £9.95 per bag, Micro-lot £12.95 per bag. Those three lines did not print a gram weight.

I am not choosing the lower figure, and I am not calling either one current until the order page is checked. A delivery fee was not in the terms extract or on the how-it-works page I used. 【Not on the pages opened for this draft: Pact’s delivery fee】

The terms say you can cancel, pause, reschedule, skip, or bring a delivery forward from the account page. They also say Pact emails 48 hours before shipping, and that changes can be made until midnight the day before the order is due to ship. A change made on the planned billing day, or after it, applies to the following shipment. The same terms describe a 14-day change-of-mind right with exceptions, including goods that deteriorate quickly, and they go on to talk about coffee in that section. I am not shortening that into “coffee can always be returned.” The terms page is the text. This is not legal advice.

A minimum number of bags was not on the pages I opened. The terms describe the plan as continuing until one side ends it. 【Not confirmed: a minimum number of Pact bags】
