The fastest way to make your pour-over taste the same every day is to weigh your coffee and water. This guide covers the features to look for in a coffee scale. It doesn't review any specific model.

## Why you need a scale

Even with the same beans and the same grind setting, small changes in the amount of coffee or water change the taste. Fix a ratio, say 15g of coffee to 240g of water, and you'll get close to the same cup every morning. I keep my grinder on one setting for daily pour-over, and that only works because the amounts are consistent too.

## Six things to check

1. **Resolution**: 0.1g steps let you match your dose precisely. At minimum get 1g resolution; ideally 0.1g.
2. **Timer**: a built-in timer lets you watch weight and time together. Some start automatically when you begin pouring.
3. **Response speed**: does the display keep up as you pour? Check reviews for this.
4. **Size**: make sure you can still read the display with a dripper and server on top.
5. **Water resistance**: spills happen, so look for water resistance or at least an easy-to-wipe surface.
6. **Power**: AA/AAA batteries or USB rechargeable. For daily use, rechargeable is less hassle.

## Rough budget tiers

- **Basic**: a scale with a timer. Plenty for a first scale.
- **Mid range**: faster response, water resistance, auto-start timers.
- **Premium**: flow-rate display, app connectivity, and more.
