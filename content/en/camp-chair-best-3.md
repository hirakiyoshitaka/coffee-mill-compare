## Helinox Chair One

Open this one first for solo camping you carry yourself. The US product table lists an assembled height of 65 cm, seat height 22.5 cm, width 52.5 cm, depth 51 cm, and weight 893 g. Packed size is 35.5 × 11 × 11 cm, and packed weight is 965 g. Capacity is 145 kg. The frame is DAC aluminum alloy, the seat fabric is 600D, and the product page lists a five-year warranty.

Packed weight is higher than assembled weight because the case is included. The US page we checked does not list a yen price. Read the store page for the price in Japan.

**Review summary (not first-hand use).** The recurring checks are a 22.5 cm seat, lower than a dining chair, a packed weight under about 1 kg, and the 145 kg rating. Helinox’s handling guide treats capacity as an even load on the frame. No star rating is copied here.

## Helinox Chair Zero

This is the second chair when grams come first. The US page lists assembled height 64 cm, seat height 16.5 cm, width 50.5 cm, depth 46 cm, and weight 509 g. Packed size is 35 × 10 × 10 cm, packed weight 531 g, capacity 120 kg. The seat fabric is 100D and the frame is DAC aluminum alloy. That page showed a price of 139.95 US dollars. Stock and the yen price change.

Against Chair One, the published gaps are 384 g assembled, 6 cm of seat height, and 25 kg of capacity. The seat sits lower. If you want a long back, look at rank 3.

**Review summary (not first-hand use).** The comparison is 531 g packed, a 16.5 cm seat, and a 120 kg rating. There is no comfort score.

## Snow Peak Take! Chair Long (LV-086)

This is the long back you unload from a car. The Japanese page lists 595 × 800 × 950 mm high in use, a seat height of 360 mm, a stored size of 100 × 150 × 1,280 mm, and a weight of 3.5 kg. The frame is laminated bamboo and aluminum alloy, the fittings are stainless steel, and the seat is No. 6 cotton canvas. The page showed ¥27,720 including tax. It does not publish a load rating. Figures from third-party inquiries are not repeated here.

The maker notes that the color differs from LV-081R because the production background changed. Check the store photo for the current color. A stored length of 1,280 mm is why this chair is not higher on a list about what you carry.

**Review summary (not first-hand use).** The checks are 3.5 kg, a stored length of 1,280 mm, a 360 mm seat, and the missing official load rating.
