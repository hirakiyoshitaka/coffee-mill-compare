## When the 2026 sales run

In the US, Black Friday falls on **Friday, November 27, 2026**, and Cyber Monday on **Monday, November 30**. Published trackers report that Amazon's Black Friday Week deal events run November 19–30. Each seller sets its own timing, so check the product page on the day. Sources are at the end of this page.

## Decide these three things before the sale

1. **How much you brew at once.** I grind about 30g at a time, for 2–3 cups. Check that the grinder you are looking at takes that much in one go.
2. **Hand or electric.** If turning a handle sounds like part of the pleasure, go hand. If the morning is a race, go electric. The trade-off is in [hand vs electric grinders](/en/articles/hand-vs-electric-coffee-mill/).
3. **Where the beans come from.** A grinder without beans on hand goes quiet. My bean arrangement is a subscription, covered below — with one shipping limit that matters for US readers.

## 1. Timemore Chestnut S3 (the grinder I use)

The hand grinder I usually use is the [Timemore Chestnut S3](/en/articles/timemore-chestnut-s3/). I grind about 30g at a time, for 2–3 cups.

From Timemore's official pages, the points worth checking when you compare:

- The burr is the S2C890 steel burr
- Adjustment is an external dial: 0.015mm per click, 90 clicks per turn, so a setting can be left in place and found again
- The handle folds down

The side-by-side with the Comandante C40 is [Comandante C40 vs Timemore](/en/articles/comandante-c40-vs-timemore/).

## 2. EPEIOS Essence Go (the light-turning one)

The second grinder I own is the EPEIOS Essence Go. My one-line impression: it turns remarkably lightly. EPEIOS describes the light start of the grind as the design goal.

From the maker's published specs:

- Adjustment runs 0–80 steps, read on a scale from the outside
- The hopper holds about 30g at most
- It weighs about 870g, and comes apart to pack into its own case

The review is [EPEIOS Essence Go](/en/articles/epeios-essence-go/). We have not confirmed a matching Amazon.com listing for this model, so this page links the review instead of a product page.

## 3. Beans, on a subscription

I kept a [Coffee Carrot (珈琲きゃろっと)](/beans/coffee-carrot/) subscription for about a year because the coffee tasted good, and ground the beans in the Chestnut S3. One limit matters here: Coffee Carrot ships only within Japan. For a US address, treat this as a record of how the setup works, and source beans locally. The shop page is in Japanese.

- **Price:** sale prices can change by the day. Read the figure on the product page before ordering

- **Seller:** the same product can be listed by several sellers. Check who sells it and who ships it
