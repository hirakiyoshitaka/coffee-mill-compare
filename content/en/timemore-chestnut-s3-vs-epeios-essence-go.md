The two hand grinders this site uses are the Timemore Chestnut S3 and the EPEIOS Essence Go. This is the only comparison on the site where both machines are ones the operator actually has: the S3 is the grinder I usually use, and the Essence Go is the one that just spins so easily. I do not use the Chestnut S5 Pro; that comparison is a review summary.

## The short answer

- **Stays on the counter for home brewing** → Timemore Chestnut S3. I usually use it, about 30g at a time, for 2–3 cups.
- **Lightest turning, and a body that packs into its case** → EPEIOS Essence Go. My one line: it just spins so easily.

## What I use

The hand grinder I usually use is the Timemore Chestnut S3. I grind about 30g at a time, for 2–3 cups.

My one-line take on the Essence Go: it just spins so easily. EPEIOS says the grinder was designed for a light start, and that matches the impression from actually turning it.

The S3's official page gives starting click ranges: espresso 1–15, moka 5–20, pour-over 50–80, French press 80–90. This site has not measured how much force either handle takes.

## Difference 1: burrs and adjustment

The S3 pairs S2C890 burrs with an external dial at 0.015mm per click, so a setting can be left in place and found again. The US official page notes the burr gap at zero is 0.075mm, and that pressing the handle down can shift the shaft slightly and cause rubbing.

The Essence Go uses 38mm burrs with 10 blades. EPEIOS says the blade count cuts friction and lightens the start of the grind. The scale runs 0–80 and reads from the outside, so the setting is visible without taking the grinder apart.

## Difference 2: weight and where it lives

The Essence Go is about 870g, and its body comes apart into a dedicated carry case. The S3's weight was not confirmed in the body of the official product page, so this page does not give one. The practical difference is design: the S3 is the counter grinder for a repeatable pour-over; the Essence Go is the one that packs away.

## Which one to pick

- The grinder I usually use, about 30g at a time for 2–3 cups → **Chestnut S3**
- You want the lightest turning, or a grinder you can take apart and carry → **Essence Go**
- The two do different jobs. If you only buy one and brew at home most mornings, the S3 is the one I use.
