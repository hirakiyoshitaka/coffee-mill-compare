Japan has a long pour-over tradition, and a few Japanese makers have tried to put that craft into a machine. This guide compares three of the most talked-about: the design-led **BALMUDA The Brew**, and the **TWINBIRD fully automatic coffee makers** (6-cup CM-D465B and 3-cup CM-D457B), developed with Mamoru Taguchi of Tokyo's Café Bach and fitted with a mill using blades made in Tsubame-Sanjo, Niigata's famous metalworking town.

To be upfront: **I have not used any of these three machines.** This is based on official specs, retailer listings, owner reviews on Kakaku.com and Amazon Japan, and a hands-on video review. Prices are as of November 1, 2026 and change often.

## Read this first if you live outside Japan

All three are **Japan-market models rated AC 100V, 50/60Hz**.

- **US/Canada (120V)**: many owners run 100V appliances on a step-down transformer. A heater of this size draws a lot of power (The Brew is rated 1,450W), so you need a transformer rated well above that.
- **UK/EU (230V)**: you must use a step-down transformer. A plug adapter alone will destroy the machine.
- Warranties are usually valid in Japan only, and manuals are in Japanese.

Amazon.co.jp ships many items internationally, and the CM-D457B is listed with international shipping on Amazon Japan. Check the listing for your country, plus import fees.

## The short answer

- **You want to watch a precise pour, and you already grind** → BALMUDA The Brew. Owners on Amazon Japan often mention the design and a clean finish. It has no built-in grinder, and it brews 3 cups.
- **Beans in, a pot for several people** → TWINBIRD CM-D465B. Kakaku.com and Amazon Japan reviews mention taste more than anything else. It is 42.5cm tall.
- **The same bean-to-cup idea for one or two people** → TWINBIRD CM-D457B. Same mill and temperatures, in a shorter 3-cup body.

I have not used these machines. The lines above summarize owner reviews that the sections below quote in more detail. If you want to watch it brew, open The Brew listing first and check the current price and stock.

## BALMUDA The Brew: a precision pour-over you'll want on display

Launched in October 2021, The Brew drips water in 0.2ml steps into a dedicated cone dripper and changes temperature for blooming, extraction, and finishing. At the end it adds a **bypass pour** that skips the coffee bed, aiming for a strong cup with a clean finish. There are three modes: Regular, Strong, and Iced. You can watch steam rise as the grounds bloom, which is a big part of its appeal.

- **What owners like**: 4.3 out of 5 on Amazon Japan (166 ratings). Owners praise the design, the pleasure of watching it brew, and the clean taste.
- **What to weigh**: Kakaku.com owner reviews average 3.45 (24 reviews), so opinions are mixed. It has no grinder, brews 3 cups max, and has no warming plate.

## TWINBIRD CM-D465B (6-cup): bean-to-cup, guided by a master roaster

TWINBIRD is based in Tsubame-Sanjo. Its fully automatic maker uses a slow flat burr mill to limit heat from friction, a six-direction shower head, and a bloom step to imitate hand pouring. You can pick coarse, medium, or fine grind and an 83°C or 90°C brew temperature (90°C suits dark roasts and iced coffee). It comes with two drippers sized for different batch sizes.

- **What owners like**: 4.53 on Kakaku.com (21 reviews) and 4.4 out of 5 on Amazon Japan (420 ratings). Taste is the most common praise.
- **What to weigh**: at 42.5cm tall it needs clearance under cabinets.

## TWINBIRD CM-D457B (3-cup): the same approach, smaller

Same mill, temperatures, and grind settings as the CM-D465B, with a 450ml capacity and a 36cm height. It has sold steadily since 2018 and ranked #7 of 1,105 coffee makers on Kakaku.com's best-seller list on November 1, 2026.

- **What owners like**: 4.46 on Kakaku.com (40 reviews) and 4.4 out of 5 on Amazon Japan (822 ratings). One owner who switched from Panasonic wrote that taste is what matters, and rated it above their old machine.
- **What to weigh**: in a [video review by Nif Coffee](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TxNSjOgK1K8), a Japanese roaster's channel, grinding 3 cups took about 4 min 16 s, and you have to measure water and beans yourself each time. Some Kakaku.com owners also find the mill tedious to clean.
