The right all-in-one machine depends less on the spec sheet and more on one question: **do you want to make espresso, or just drink it?** This guide matches six current models to that answer.

We have not used these machines. Summaries come from makers' specs and published reviews, linked below. Prices are late-October-2026 snapshots.

## Quick picks

- **Best if you want zero effort and lots of drinks:** **De'Longhi Rivelia**
- **Best for iced coffee and cold brew lovers:** **De'Longhi Eletta Explore**
- **Best hands-off "real" espresso:** **Breville Oracle Jet**
- **Best for learning with a coach:** **Breville Barista Touch Impress**
- **Best value guided machine in the UK:** **De'Longhi La Specialista Touch**
- **Best for hands-on milk steaming:** **Breville Barista Express Impress**

## Breville Oracle Jet

US $1,999.95 list · UK about £1,699 (Sage)

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Automatic grind, dose and tamp | Large and heavy (about 12.6 kg) |
| 58 mm portafilter, 45 grind settings, Baratza burrs | Grind knob can be sticky (Serious Eats) |
| Hands-free milk with temperature and milk-type settings | Static mess reported (TechRadar) |
| Cold brew and cold espresso presets | Highest price of the six |

**Review summary:** Serious Eats liked its compact auto-tamped puck and latte-art-ready foam; TechRadar praised the extraction feedback; Forbes Vetted rated it 3.8.

[Breville US](https://www.breville.com/en-us/product/bes985) · [Sage UK](https://www.sageappliances.com/en-gb)

## Breville Barista Touch Impress

US $1,499.95 list · UK £1,199.95 (Sage BES882)

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Touchscreen coaching with shot feedback | Expensive for a semi-automatic |
| Assisted Impress tamp, Baratza burrs | Experienced users may find it limiting (Serious Eats) |
| Auto MilQ with plant-milk settings | Earlier version had a durability failure in Serious Eats testing |
| Cold espresso and cold brew | |

**Review summary:** TechRadar calls it an excellent option for beginners; Serious Eats measured cold espresso at 102°F and a cold brew in under two minutes.

[Breville US](https://www.breville.com/en-us/product/bes881) · [Sage UK](https://www.sageappliances.com/en-gb/product/bes882)

## Breville Barista Express Impress

US $799.95 list (seen at $649.95 on sale at Macy's) · UK £599.95

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Assisted tamp with dose feedback | Manual milk steaming only |
| PID temperature control, 25 grind settings | No touchscreen, no cold drinks |
| Lightest Breville here (about 10.3 kg) | 54 mm rather than 58 mm portafilter |

**Review summary:** the modern version of Breville's best-selling grinder machine — the choice for people who enjoy the process.

[Breville US](https://www.breville.com/en-us/product/bes876) · [Sage UK](https://www.sageappliances.com/en-gb)

## De'Longhi Eletta Explore

UK £899.99–£999.99 · US prices vary by retailer

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Cold brew in about 5 minutes | Large, deep footprint |
| 50+ drinks, hot and cold milk | Premium only worth it if you drink cold coffee (Balance Journal) |
| Bean Adapt grind tuning via app, four profiles | Single fixed hopper |

**Review summary:** BBC Good Food 4.5/5 (glossy crema, user-friendly); Balance Journal 7.5/10.

[De'Longhi US](https://www.delonghi.com/en-us) · [De'Longhi UK](https://www.delonghi.com/en-gb)

## De'Longhi Rivelia

UK £649.99–£749.99 · US prices vary by retailer

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Two swappable 250 g bean hoppers | Noisy grinder (The Guardian) |
| Up to 16 drinks, user profiles | Milk texture could be better (The Guardian) |
| One-touch automatic milk | No cold brew |

**Review summary:** The Guardian's best bean-to-cup under £1,000; Coffee Machine Reviews UK's best overall.

[De'Longhi US](https://www.delonghi.com/en-us) · [De'Longhi UK](https://www.delonghi.com/en-gb)

## De'Longhi La Specialista Touch

UK £699.99

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Guided touchscreen, auto LatteArt milk with 5 foam levels | Only 15 grind settings |
| Large accessory kit included | Not one-touch; more cleaning than bean-to-cup |
| Relatively compact | Integrated grinder limits upgrades |

**Review summary:** BBC Good Food 5/5; Coffee Machine Reviews UK scored it 9.6 for milk drinks and 9.4 for value.

[De'Longhi UK](https://www.delonghi.com/en-gb) · [De'Longhi US](https://www.delonghi.com/en-us)

## How to decide in 30 seconds

1. Don't want to touch a portafilter? → **Rivelia** (or **Eletta Explore** if you drink iced coffee).
2. Want real espresso but no tamping? → **Oracle Jet**.
3. Want to learn, with help? → **Barista Touch Impress** or **La Specialista Touch**.
4. Want full control of milk? → **Barista Express Impress**.
