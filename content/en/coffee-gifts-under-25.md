The cone to start pour-over with one cup. The 01 size brews 1–2 cups, and the clear plastic body is light and hard to break. It takes the 01-size V60 paper, sold separately. ## 2. Hario V60 plastic dripper 02 (VD-02T)

The same cone in the larger size: the 02 brews 1–4 cups, so it covers one big mug or coffee for two. Pick the 01 above for a single cup every time, and the 02 when the cup is bigger or shared. It takes the 02-size V60 paper, sold separately. We have not used it.

## 3. Hario V60 paper filters 02, 100 (VCF-02-100W)

A consumable is the easiest gift for someone who already brews V60: it always runs out. One pack holds 100 sheets, and the 02 size fits the 02 dripper and brews 1–4 cups. White paper is the bleached version; Hario also sells the same size in natural brown (VCF-02-100M). Match the paper to the dripper size first: 02 paper fits a 02 dripper, not a 01. The three Hario pieces to start with — paper, scale, and kettle — are in the [Hario V60 pour-over starter](/en/articles/hario-v60-pour-over-starter/).

<p class="affiliate-html"><a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=HARIO+VCF-02-100W&tag=ouchidougub03-20">Search the Hario VCF-02-100W on Amazon.com</a> (ad)</p>

## 4. Hario Pegasus dripper 01 (PED-01-T)

Not a V60 at all: a flat-bottom trapezoid with the V60's spiral ribs. Hario's page says its two small holes restrict the flow, so the bed blooms more readily and the taste repeats more easily. The 01 brews a single cup, and it is the smallest and lightest of the three Pegasus sizes. Note that it needs its own paper — the trapezoid Pegasus paper, not V60 paper. We have not used it.

<p class="affiliate-html"><a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=HARIO+Pegasus+dripper+01&tag=ouchidougub03-20">Search the Hario Pegasus dripper 01 on Amazon.com</a> (ad)</p>

## 5. AeroPress paper micro-filters, 350

For someone who already owns an AeroPress: a pack of 350 paper micro-filters, about a year's worth at one press a day. AeroPress's own page says the Standard filter fits the AeroPress Original, AeroPress Clear, and AeroPress Go, and does not fit the AeroPress XL, which has its own larger filter. The AeroPress itself is in [coffee gifts under $50](/en/articles/coffee-gifts-under-50/). We have not used these filters.

## How to choose among the five

| They already have | Start with |
|---|---|
| No brewing gear yet | The V60 plastic dripper 01 |
| A V60 02 dripper, paper running low | The V60 paper filters 02, 100 sheets |
| They brew for two | The V60 plastic dripper 02 |
| A V60 and they want a second, more forgiving brewer | The Pegasus dripper 01 (with its own trapezoid paper) |
| An AeroPress Original, Clear, or Go | The AeroPress micro-filters, 350 |

Match the paper to the brewer before ordering: V60 paper fits the V60 cone, Pegasus paper fits the Pegasus, and AeroPress Standard filters do not fit the AeroPress XL.

Prices and stock change, and the same product can be listed by several sellers. Check the seller and the delivery date before ordering.
