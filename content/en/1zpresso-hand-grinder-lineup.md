1Zpresso model names sit close together, which is why people stall on which hand grinder to buy. This page stays with five lines the maker already separates by job: a travel Q, the smaller-burr X-Ultra, the pour-over ZP6, the all-method K-Ultra, and the espresso-focused J-Ultra.

We have not used a 1Zpresso. There are no tasting notes on this page. Every number is what the official page showed when we opened it on October 31, 2026. Those pages get revised, so open the same page again before you order.

If the grinder is going to live at home for daily pour-over, the one this site still points to first is the [Timemore Chestnut S5 Pro](/en/articles/timemore-chestnut-s5-pro/). Use the 1Zpresso list when you already know which job the grinder has to do.

## The short answer

- **A lighter bag, and drip for one or two people** → Q2 S. If the gram count matters more than a folding handle, look at the Q Air
- **A smaller burr, with drip and espresso both adjusted from the outside** → X-Ultra
- **Pour-over, on a burr the maker describes as producing fewer fines** → ZP6 Special
- **One grinder that has to cover more than one brew method** → K-Ultra
- **The finest espresso clicks 1Zpresso publishes** → J-Ultra

The official chooser still lists the J, JX-Pro S, JE-Plus, and X-Pro S, and it includes a click chart. This page stays with the five lines whose jobs are easiest to tell apart.

## Who each one is for

### Taking it with you: the Q

1Zpresso describes the Q Series as the smallest and lightest line in the range. Capacity is 15–20g, which suits drip for one cup or two people. You adjust it from the inside. The scale is not a dial you can read from the outside.

Choose the Q2 S (465g) when you want a folding handle. Choose the Q Air (365g) when you want the lowest weight. The Q Air handle does not fold. On the official table, the Q Air body and catch cup are plastic.

### A smaller burr, at home or in a bag: X-Ultra

The X-Ultra weighs 590g, holds 25–30g, and has a folding handle and a magnetic catch cup. The maker says the smaller heptagonal burr takes less force. Adjustment is external, at 12.5 microns per click, and the grinder is positioned for both drip and espresso. This site has no tasting that compares those two uses.

### Fewer fines in a pour-over: ZP6 Special

The ZP6 Special uses a 48mm hexagonal stainless burr. The official description says it produces fewer fines than 1Zpresso's other grinders. Each click is 22 microns, a wider step than the K-Ultra or the J-Ultra, so the grinder sits close to a drip tool. Weight is 700g. The product page says Carlos Medina used one at the 2023 World Brewers Cup. We have not checked that competition record.

### One grinder across brew methods: K-Ultra

The K-Ultra is the model 1Zpresso places in the all-methods slot. The external dial is 100 clicks per turn and 20 microns per click, and the manual says you can keep turning past one full rotation. Capacity is 35–40g and weight is 700g. The burr is heptagonal. The product page opened on October 31, 2026 did not give the burr diameter in millimeters. Espresso sits inside its range. If you are comparing click size alone, the J-Ultra's published step is finer.

### Espresso clicks: J-Ultra

The J-Ultra is the 8-micron step that 1Zpresso calls the finest in its range. The burr is a coated 48mm, and the maker describes the grinder as built for espresso. Weight is 670g, capacity is 35–40g, and the catch cup is magnetic. A finer click does not remove the work of grinding espresso by hand every morning. If that is the plan, read [how to choose an electric coffee grinder](/en/articles/electric-coffee-grinder-guide/) and [hand vs electric grinders](/en/articles/hand-vs-electric-coffee-mill/). The only hand grinder whose official espresso range this site has checked is the [Chestnut S5 Pro](/en/articles/timemore-chestnut-s5-pro/). This article does not copy a recommended J-Ultra click number.

## A click number does not travel to another grinder

"Setting 6" is a different grind once the burr and the width of each click change. Inside 1Zpresso, the Q series step of 0.025mm, the X-Ultra's 12.5 microns, the ZP6's 22 microns, the K-Ultra's 20 microns, and the J-Ultra's 8 microns are separate scales. The Chestnut S5 Pro's 15 microns per click is a different scale again. The order of grind size by brew method is in the [coffee grind size guide](/en/articles/coffee-grind-size-guide/).
