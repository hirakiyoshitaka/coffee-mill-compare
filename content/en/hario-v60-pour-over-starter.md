Pour-over starts with three pieces besides the dripper itself: paper that fits the cone, a scale that weighs and times, and a kettle that pours thin. These are the three Hario V60 pieces for that job — the 02 paper filters, the V60 drip scale, and the Buono kettle.

This is a review summary. We have not used any of the three pieces: each section below follows the maker's page and published reviews, and most links go to Amazon.com search results, so compare sellers and check the price and stock on the day you buy.

## 1. Paper filters: HARIO VCF-02-100W

The V60 02 paper fits the conical 02 dripper and brews 1–4 cups. One box holds 100 sheets, so restocking comes up less often than with a 50-sheet box. Match the paper to the dripper shape first: a conical dripper takes conical paper, and a wave dripper takes wave paper.

## 2. Drip scale: HARIO VSTN-2000B

The scale weighs the beans and the poured water, and its timer watches the bloom and the drawdown, so the same recipe repeats more easily. It weighs 2–2,000g; the 0.1g display covers 2–200g, so weigh the beans on their own for the fine reading. The timer runs to 99 minutes 59 seconds and starts by hand. It runs on two AAA batteries, and measures 120 × 190 × 29mm.

## 3. Kettle: HARIO VKB-120HSV

The Buono is the gooseneck kettle the V60 was designed around: its slim spout controls where the water lands and how fast it falls. The practical capacity is 800 mL, with 1.2 L to the brim — heat up to the practical line, not to the brim. The body and lid are stainless steel; the handle and lid knob are phenol resin. It is made in Japan, and measures W274 × D144 × H147mm.

Hario USA's page for this kettle lists size, capacity, materials, and origin, but no heat source — check your stove type with Hario before buying. There is no temperature control on the kettle itself, so check the water with a thermometer if you want a target number. How to choose a kettle in general — spout, temperature control, capacity, heat source — is in the [gooseneck kettle guide](/en/articles/gooseneck-kettle-guide/).
