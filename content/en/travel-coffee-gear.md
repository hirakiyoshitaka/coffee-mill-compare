## What not to pack

- **Beans ground at home for the whole trip.** Ground coffee goes stale faster than whole beans, and a hand grinder is the point of this list. Grind each morning instead.
- **A glass brewer with no case.** Glass breaks in a bag. Every brewer on this page is plastic, steel, or silicone, or packs into its own mug.
- **A full-size kettle.** On a trip, hot water comes from the stay or the camp stove. A gooseneck is for the counter at home — how to choose one is in [how to choose a gooseneck kettle](/en/articles/gooseneck-kettle-guide/).
