I am a 58-year-old office worker. Once I started using a kettle made for pour-over, the thin spout meant fewer spills than with an ordinary kettle. I am not naming the brand, and I am not describing a favorite water temperature.

The three below are review summaries of premium gooseneck kettles, using only figures from official pages opened on November 2, 2026. I am not describing how they pour in my hand.

1. Brewista Artisan gooseneck 1.0 L. A review summary. The Japan page is 100 V
2. HARIO Electric Kettle Lyra. A review summary. The Japan page is 100 V
3. Fellow Stagg EKG. A review summary. The official page I used is a US voltage

## 1. Brewista Artisan 1.0 L, a review summary

From [Brewista Japan's product page](https://brewista.jp/products/brewista-artisan-gooseneck-kettle-1-0l).

- The listed price is 29,700 yen. Power is 100 V, 950 W. Capacity 1.0 L
- 40°C to 100°C in 1°C steps. The page also lists hold, a fast boil, and a count-up timer
- Brewista calls the spout an extra-fine gooseneck
- Made in China. Household use. The shop FAQ says it is not for use overseas
- The dimension line reads "base W230*D1950*H40 mm", and the depth figure is hard to trust as printed, so I am not stating a millimeter depth. The body line reads W310*D150*H195. Check the diagram on the page

[Search for the Brewista Artisan 1.0 L on Amazon.co.jp](https://www.amazon.co.jp/s?k=Brewista+Artisan+1.0L&tag=stocknotelife-22)

## 2. HARIO Electric Kettle Lyra, a review summary

From [HARIO's Lyra page](https://www.hario.com/product/new/ECK.html). Model numbers ECK-80-W / B.

- Suggested retail price 23,000 yen, 25,300 yen including tax
- AC 100 V, 50/60 Hz. 1,200 W. Maximum 0.8 L, minimum 0.3 L
- About W280×D157×H210 mm. About 1.1 kg. HARIO also says the body is about 0.53 kg. Cord about 1.2 m
- Gooseneck spout. 38°C to 100°C in 1°C steps. Boil, 15-minute hold, KEEP, five presets, a stopwatch, and a timer
- Made in China

[Search for the HARIO Lyra on Amazon.co.jp](https://www.amazon.co.jp/s?k=HARIO+Lyra+ECK-80&tag=stocknotelife-22)

## 3. Fellow Stagg EKG, a review summary

From [Fellow's US Stagg EKG page](https://fellowproducts.com/products/stagg-ekg-electric-pour-over-kettle).

- Capacity 0.9 L (30 oz). Temperature 104–212°F / 40–100°C. Fellow calls the spout a precision gooseneck
- Power 110–120 V, 1,000–1,200 W, 50–60 Hz. Plug Type-B, for the US and Canada
- Size 282×172×196 mm. Weight 1,400 g including the base. Cord 76.2 cm
- That page does not say the kettle is a Japan 100 V model. Check the rating on the unit you would buy. I am not judging whether a converter is appropriate

[Search for the Fellow Stagg EKG on Amazon.co.jp](https://www.amazon.co.jp/s?k=Fellow+Stagg+EKG&tag=stocknotelife-22)

## Besides the thin spout

My own experience stops at "a thin spout, so fewer spills." The three kettles are ones whose makers describe that shape. For a Japanese household outlet, open the 100 V Brewista and HARIO first, and read the voltage on any Fellow listing. Capacities are 0.8 L, 0.9 L, and 1.0 L. I am not guessing a full-kettle weight where the maker did not publish the kettle alone.
